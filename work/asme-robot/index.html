Autonomous Waste-Collection Robot

As project lead, I took a 60+ member team through a full design cycle, from field schematics to competition day, on a robot that collects, sorts, and disposes of waste in a mock city.

Clean up a mock city, autonomously

ASME’s 2025/26 Student Design Competition asked for a robot that could navigate a mock city playfield (streets, buildings, a hill), collect waste bins, sort them, and deliver them to a dumpsite, over inclines and rough terrain.
